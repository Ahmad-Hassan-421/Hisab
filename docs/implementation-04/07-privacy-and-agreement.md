# Privacy Policy and acknowledgment

The app and server use the same [policy document](../../hisab-server/src/main/resources/policies/privacy-policy.json). It describes the data Hisab currently handles, what is shared with participants, and the roles of MongoDB Atlas, Render, Brevo, and GitHub. Android bundles a copy and can read the latest published copy from the public `/privacy/policy` endpoint. About, Settings, and signup provide access to the notice.

Signup starts with an unchecked acknowledgment and sends the version shown to the user. The server requires that version and records the time. Existing accounts remain unchanged until they explicitly acknowledge the current notice. They can sign in to review it, open their profile, submit a data request, and sign out. Shared ledgers and other protected routes require the current acknowledgment. Pending requests for access, correction, and deletion are stored with a private reference and require an authorized operator to review and fulfill them. Duplicate pending requests of the same type reuse the same reference.

`version` identifies the exact published text. For an editorial change, update `version` and `effectiveDate` and retain `acknowledgmentVersion`. For a change that requires a new acknowledgment after review, advance both versions. The server records the published version, acknowledgment version, timestamp, and history on the existing account. Repeated acceptance records once. Preserve prior published texts and agreement history when releasing a revision.

Before publication, the owner should confirm Hantalyze's legal identity and private privacy contact, the applicable jurisdictions and eligibility rules, retention and backup periods, and any required disclosures about third-party processing and transfers. The owner also needs to designate who monitors `privacy_data_requests`, verifies identities, responds to users, and determines what can be removed from shared records. Confirm the effective date with the rollout. Review this notice against the applicable rules before treating it as the final published policy. [ICO guidance on drafting privacy information](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/the-right-to-be-informed/how-should-we-draft-our-privacy-information/)

Coordinate release and backend deployment. Older Android builds can sign in and reach the update service, and the updated build provides the current acknowledgment flow before shared operations.
